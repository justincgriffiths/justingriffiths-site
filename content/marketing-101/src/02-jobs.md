# 2 · How many jobs live in marketing

**Big question:** when someone says they "work in marketing," what do they actually do all day? About 20 minutes.

&#91;embedded content: marketing jobs · 5 families × 4 funnel stages\]

Each bar is a family of jobs, stretched across the funnel stages it mostly serves. Strategy and measurement run the full width; the rest each own a stretch.

## Words to know

| Term | Plain meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product marketing | Decides who a product is for and how it's described | Writing the launch page for a new iPhone feature |
| Paid media | Attention the brand pays for, ad by ad | Instagram ads, Google search ads |
| Programmatic | Ads bought by software in real-time auctions | A banner that follows you across news sites |
| Retail media | Ads sold by a store, inside the store's own site | Sponsored results on Amazon |
| CRM / lifecycle | Talking to people who already bought | The "you left this in your cart" email |
| Attribution | Giving credit for a sale to the ads that came before it | "This sale came from the TikTok ad" |
| Incrementality | The sales an ad *caused*, beyond what would have happened anyway | Turn ads off in some cities, compare |

## Why it matters in marketing

One campaign touches several bars at once. When a number moves, the map tells you which team owns that stage, and so who to ask.

## How it connects to business

The measurement bar is applied econometrics on ad spend: it decides whether the money spent in every other bar was worth it. That makes it the family closest to an economics degree.

## Best vs worst

|  | Best: Airbnb | Worst: eBay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What they did | At the end of Q1 2020 (COVID), suspended nearly all performance marketing, and only resumed it later at a reduced level | Paid for Google ads on its own name ("ebay"), the people who were already searching for it |
| What happened | In its IPO filing, Airbnb said 98% of Q2 2020 traffic was unpaid or direct | eBay's own economists switched the ads off in a field experiment: brand-keyword ads had "no measurable short-term benefits" (NBER 2014; *Econometrica* 2015) |
| Which bar | Measurement showed which media bar was paying for itself | Measurement found a media bar paying for clicks that were free |
| Ask | If 98% of visitors came anyway, what had the ads been buying? | Why might the ad still have looked like it was working? |

Sources: [Airbnb SEC correspondence, 2020](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000095012320010023/filename1.htm), [Blake, Nosko & Tadelis, NBER w20171](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20171).

## Check yourself

A brand wants to know whether its ads *caused* sales or just took credit for them. Which family runs that test, and what is the test called?

**Answer:** measurement, with an incrementality test: show ads to one group, hold them back from a matched group, compare.
