## How every session runs

Same five beats every time, so the format never needs explaining. Short on time? Do beats 1, 4 and 5 (15 minutes).

| Minutes | Beat | What the student does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | 1. The picture | Says what they notice before reading a word |
| 3–8 | 2. Words to know | Picks the newest word and uses it in a sentence |
| 8–15 | 3. Why it matters | Explains the idea back in one sentence, for marketing and for the business |
| 15–25 | 4. Best vs worst | Guesses what went wrong before seeing the result |
| 25–30 | 5. Check yourself | Answers from memory, then links it to the thesis |
