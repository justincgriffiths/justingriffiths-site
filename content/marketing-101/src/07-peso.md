# 7 · Paid, earned, shared, owned

**Big question:** when a creator you watch mentions a brand, who paid for that? About 25 minutes.

&#91;embedded content: paid to earned to shared to paid · one creator moment\]

Media splits four ways, often called the PESO model (Gini Dietrich). Creators and streamers are where all four meet: one sponsored moment can turn into an earned take, a shared clip, and a paid boost.

## Words to know

| Type | Example on a stream or creator channel | Who controls it | How it is measured |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paid | "This stream is sponsored by…", a paid integration, a pre-roll ad | Brand pays, so brand controls the message | CPM, promo-code redemptions, tracked links |
| Earned | The creator picks up a product they like, unpaid, and talks about it | Creator; the brand cannot buy it | Mentions, share of voice, search lift |
| Shared | Fans clip the moment and repost it; chat spams the brand name | The audience | Reach, shares, clip views |
| Owned | The brand's own channel, Discord, email list | Brand, fully | Followers, open rates, repeat visits |

Two more terms. **Spark Ads** (TikTok) and **partnership ads** (Meta) let a brand pay to boost a creator's own post. **Disclosure:** in the US, the [FTC's influencer guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) says a paid relationship must be disclosed, and on a livestream the disclosure "should be repeated periodically."

## Why it matters in marketing

Earned media is the most believed and the least controlled. Paid is the reverse. Most creator marketing is an attempt to buy a little of the first with the second.

## How it connects to business

A creator's promo code is both a tracking device and a discount only that creator's audience gets: price discrimination and measurement in one string of letters. Undisclosed paid posts are a legal risk, not only a reputational one.

|  | Best: Ocean Spray and Nathan Apodaca | Worst: Fyre Festival |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What they did | In 2020 Apodaca filmed himself skateboarding to work, drinking Ocean Spray Cran-Raspberry and lip-syncing to Fleetwood Mac's "Dreams". Unpaid | December 12, 2016: Kendall Jenner, Emily Ratajkowski and other paid influencers posted the same orange-tile promo |
| What happened | "Dreams" re-entered the Billboard Hot 100 at No. 21 on October 17, 2020. Ocean Spray gave him a new pickup truck | Jenner was reportedly paid $250,000–$275,000; only Ratajkowski used #ad. The festival collapsed in 2017, and Jenner later paid a $90,000 settlement |
| PESO type | Earned, then shared at huge scale; the brand's reply was owned | Paid, dressed up as earned |
| Ask | Ocean Spray didn't plan this. What did it do right after? | Why did hiding the payment make the posts work better at first, and worse later? |
