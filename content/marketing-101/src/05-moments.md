# 5 · Moments of truth

**Big question:** when does a shopper actually decide? About 25 minutes.

Read left to right: an ad, then research online (ZMOT), then the choice at the shelf (FMOT), then using it at home (SMOT). A review posted after your SMOT becomes part of the next shopper's ZMOT: that is the loop.

## Words to know

| Term | Plain meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moment of truth | A point where the shopper's opinion is formed | Opening the box |
| ZMOT (Zero Moment) | Research after seeing an ad, before reaching the store | Reading reviews, watching an unboxing |
| FMOT (First Moment) | The seconds at the shelf when a brand gets picked; P&G put it at 3–7 seconds (P&G's own claim) | Grabbing one shampoo out of twenty |
| SMOT (Second Moment) | Using it at home, delighted or not | The first week with new headphones |
| Messy middle | Looping between widening your options and narrowing them | Ten tabs open, comparing |
| Loyalty loop | A happy buyer skips the search next time, and their review shapes others' | Reordering the same brand without looking |

## Why it matters in marketing

Each moment is won by a different channel: search and reviews at ZMOT, packaging and placement at FMOT, the product itself at SMOT. Knowing which moment decides a category says where the budget belongs.

## How it connects to business

SMOT is the moment no ad can buy: the product decides whether the marketing paid off. A good SMOT also feeds the next shopper's ZMOT for free; a bad one does the opposite.

## Best vs worst

|  | Best: Warby Parker Home Try-On | Worst: Samsung Galaxy Note 7 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What they did | Customers picked five frames on the website and tried them at home, free of charge (the program ended in 2025) | Launched in August 2016 with a full ad and retail push |
| What happened | The "home" moment came before paying: you lived with the glasses, then bought | Batteries caught fire. More than 2.5 million phones recalled; production stopped October 11, 2016; recall costs at least $5.3 billion |
| Which moment | Pulled SMOT ahead of the purchase | Every earlier moment worked; SMOT failed, in public |
| Ask | What risk does trying at home take away from the shopper? | What does a failed SMOT do to the next shopper's ZMOT? |
