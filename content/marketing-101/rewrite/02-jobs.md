# 2 · How many jobs live in marketing

**Big question:** when someone says they "work in marketing," what do they actually do all day? About 20 minutes.

## Words to know

| Term | Plain meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product marketing | You decide who a product is for and how to describe it | You write the launch page for a new iPhone feature |
| Paid media | You pay for attention, ad by ad | Instagram ads, Google search ads |
| Programmatic | You buy ads through software in real-time auctions | A banner that follows you across news sites |
| Retail media | You buy ads from a store on its own site | Sponsored results on Amazon |
| CRM / lifecycle | You talk to people who already bought | The "you left this in your cart" email |
| Attribution | You give credit for a sale to the ads that came before it | "This sale came from the TikTok ad" |
| Incrementality | You measure the sales an ad *caused*, beyond what would have happened anyway | You turn ads off in some cities and compare |

## Why it matters in marketing

One campaign touches several bars at once. When a number moves, the map shows you which team owns that stage and who to ask.

## How it connects to business

The measurement bar is applied econometrics on ad spend. You work out whether the money spent in every other bar was worth it. That makes measurement the family closest to an economics degree.

## Best vs worst

|  | Best: Airbnb | Worst: eBay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What they did | At the end of Q1 2020 (COVID), Airbnb suspended nearly all performance marketing. It only resumed later at a reduced level. | eBay paid for Google ads on its own name ("ebay"), reaching people already searching for it. |
| What happened | In its IPO filing, Airbnb said 98% of Q2 2020 traffic was unpaid or direct. | eBay's own economists switched the ads off in a field experiment. Brand-keyword ads had "no measurable short-term benefits" (NBER 2014; *Econometrica* 2015). |
| Which bar | Measurement showed which media bar was paying for itself. | Measurement found a media bar paying for clicks that were free. |
| Ask | If 98% of visitors came anyway, what do you think the ads had been buying? | Why do you think the ad might still have looked like it was working? |

Sources: [Airbnb SEC correspondence, 2020](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000095012320010023/filename1.htm), [Blake, Nosko & Tadelis, NBER w20171](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20171).

## Check yourself

You want to know whether a brand's ads *caused* sales or just took credit for them. Which family would you ask to run that test, and what is the test called?

**Answer:** measurement, with an incrementality test. You show ads to one group, hold them back from a matched group, and compare.
