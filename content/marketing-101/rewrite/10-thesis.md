# 10 · Bring it to the thesis

**Big question:** what does marketing say about [THESIS TOPIC]? Allow about 15–30 minutes, ideally after you finish the other nine.

&#91;embedded content: thesis lenses · 3 questions\]

You can use the funnel from session 3, the attention market from session 4, or the moments from session 5.

## Talk-through

| Min | Prompt |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Say your thesis topic in one sentence. Who are the "customers" in it? |
| 5–15 | Choose one lens. Sketch it on paper for your topic. |
| 15–25 | Which lens gives you data you could actually collect? |
| 25–30 | Pick the one you will bring to the next session. |

## One word to keep from each session

| Session | Word | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Shift | Marketing moves the demand curve; a sale only slides along it. |
| 2 | Incrementality | What did an ad cause beyond what would have happened anyway? |
