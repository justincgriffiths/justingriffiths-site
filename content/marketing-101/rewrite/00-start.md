# Marketing 101: ten talk-through sessions


You have ten sessions of 15–30 minutes, one idea each. Each session starts with a picture, then words to learn and why the idea matters. You compare one brand that got it right with one that got it wrong.

## How every session runs

You follow the same five beats each time, so you do not need to relearn the format. Short on time? Do beats 1, 4 and 5 (15 minutes).

| Minutes | Beat | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | 1. The picture | Say what you notice before reading a word. |
| 3–8 | 2. Words to know | Pick the newest word and use it in a sentence. |
| 8–15 | 3. Why it matters | Explain the idea back in one sentence, for marketing and for the business. |
| 15–25 | 4. Best vs worst | Guess what went wrong before you see the result. |
| 25–30 | 5. Check yourself | Answer from memory, then connect it to your thesis. |
