  • Regional Dynamics: The analysis highlights how Saudi Arabia and Turkey's engagement with Iran during high-stakes periods (e.g., Syria) provided opportunities for more nuanced negotiations, particularly as they sought to position themselves in regional power dynamics.

  • Historical Context: The argument underscores Iran's internal decision-making during periods of severe sanctions (e.g., 2015-2018) as a critical factor in its willingness to engage diplomatically. This context is crucial for understanding how sanctions might vary in their impact and effectiveness.
