US/Iran conflict: nuclear negotiations, sanctions regime, and the 2025-2026 escalation

US/Iran conflict: nuclear negotiations, sanctions regime, and the 2025-2026 escalation

April 30, 2026·3 min read·6.8Converged

Core Arguments

  1. Strengths:

    • Sanctions have not substantially halted Iran's nuclear program despite their implementation.
    • There is no clear evidence that sanctions compelled Iran to change its behavior regarding nuclear proliferation, as it continued to engage in covert activities such as enriching uranium up to 60%.
    • Economic impacts of sanctions are significant but do not directly affect the core aspects of Iran’s nuclear program.

  2. Weaknesses:

    • The claim that "sanctions have been ineffective" is challenged by acknowledging that economic pressures did impact Iranian compliance with non-proliferation norms, albeit in limited ways.
    • The counter-argument suggests sanctions have not deterred Iran from pursuing nuclear activities covertly, but this oversimplifies the complexity of Iran's internal and regional dynamics.

  3. Surviving Critiques:

    • The effectiveness of sanctions on economic fronts, which can impact overall national stability and decision-making in matters like nuclear proliferation.
    • Evidence of Iran's continued pursuit of a covert nuclear program despite sanction-imposed restrictions.

Counterargument Summary

The counter-argument highlights the challenges posed by Iran’s ability to maintain its nuclear activities under severe economic pressures. It underscores that while sanctions have strangled Iranian finances and imposed significant costs, they do not compel Iran to alter its strategic course regarding nuclear proliferation. The argument provides evidence of Iran’s continued pursuit of uranium enrichment up to 60%, indicating a covert evasion of international norms.

To strengthen this thesis, the original argument would need stronger evidence showing:

  1. Specific Evidence Linking Sanctions to Iranian Nuclear Activities: Detailed data on how sanctions have impacted specific segments of Iran's nuclear program.
  2. Demonstrating Compliance with International Norms: Clear examples or expert testimonies indicating that Iran adhered strictly to international norms regarding nuclear activities after the JCPOA.

Unresolved Questions

  1. How Sanctions Affect Nuclear Activities in Specific Facilities?
    • Research is needed on how sanctions have impacted specific facilities, such as those involved in uranium enrichment and plutonium production.
  2. Impact of Regional Dynamics: Understanding the influence of regional dynamics like diplomatic outreach by UAE, Israel, and Saudi Arabia on Iran’s nuclear decisions during 2025-2026.
  3. Economic Resilience of Iranian Entities: Analyzing whether sanctions have significantly weakened key entities within Iran's nuclear supply chain.

Essay Angles

  1. "The Sanctions Regime’s Success: A Misunderstood Economic Impact on Nuclear Policies": Discuss how sanctions, despite their economic challenges, did not impede Iran's core capabilities in maintaining a covert nuclear program.
  2. "Regional Dynamics and Escalation Management: Beyond US-Sanctions Focus": Explore the role of regional actors like UAE, Israel, and Saudi Arabia in influencing Iran’s strategic decisions during the 2025-2026 period.
  3. "The Role of Domestic Politics in Nuclear Decisions: The Case of Ebrahim Raisi's Presidency": Analyze how internal political shifts under different presidents (Rouhani vs. Raisi) contributed to Iran’s nuclear policies and external perceptions.

Conclusion

The counterargument convincingly illustrates the limitations and complexities within sanctions' effectiveness on Iran's nuclear program. To fortify this thesis, it is essential to provide concrete evidence linking sanctions to specific aspects of Iran's nuclear activities and to account for regional dynamics and domestic political shifts. These areas of research will offer deeper insights into the nuances of the 2025-2026 escalation and the role of sanctions in shaping Iran’s nuclear policies.
