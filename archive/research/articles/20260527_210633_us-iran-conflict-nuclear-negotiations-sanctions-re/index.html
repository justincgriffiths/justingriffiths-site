However, this claim does not account for other significant factors that may have contributed to the escalations.

Military Intentions: Military intentions played a role in the US-Iran conflict but are difficult to substantiate without direct evidence from military or intelligence sources. For instance, any official statements of aggressive plans by either side would be crucial to support this claim.

  • Evidence Needed: The research should provide specific examples or testimonies from credible sources such as former officials, military leaders, or intelligence agencies that indicate the presence of military intentions.
