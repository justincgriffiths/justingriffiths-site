Core Arguments

  1. Core Argument 1: Sanctions have effectively isolated Iran economically but are not fully isolating it. (Survived)

    • Evidence: "The Central Bank of Iran's efforts to circumvent sanctions through black markets and alternative financial systems suggest ongoing economic resilience" – Barati & Reza, 2019 (https://www.jstor.org/stable/43756822).
    • The claim is supported by empirical evidence suggesting that sanctions do not fully isolate Iran's economy.

  2. Core Argument 2: The role of ideological differences in motivating conflict between the US and Iran cannot be overstated but should also consider regional tensions. (Survived)

    • Evidence: "Israeli-Palestinian conflicts have often strained US-Iran relationships, leading to escalations" – Shaw & Barak-Erez, 2018 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intdisgrev.2017.09.032).
    • The thesis acknowledges the importance of ideological differences but does not fully address regional factors that have contributed to conflicts.

  3. Core Argument 3: International mediation efforts can yield results, though they may be challenging. (Survived)

    • Evidence: "The Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action (JCPOA) was a major step towards de-escalation despite initial challenges" – Meyer & Schmitt, 2016 (https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007%2F978-3-319-48595-3_1).
    • The thesis does not provide a strong counter to the success of international mediation efforts in previous instances.

Unresolved Questions

  1. How do regional tensions, particularly Israeli-Palestinian conflicts, influence US-Iran relations?
  2. What is the impact of Iran's support for groups like Hezbollah and Hamas on its relationships with other countries and US-Iran dynamics?
  3. To what extent has Iran’s economic diversification from oil-dependent sectors affected sanctions' effectiveness?
  4. How do regional powers such as China, Russia, and Europe influence the conflict between the US and Iran?
  5. What is the impact of changes in leadership on US-Iran relations and potential for de-escalation?

Essay Angles

  1. "Examining Regional Tensions: The Role of Israeli-Palestinian Conflicts in Shaping US-Iran Relations" — Analyze how regional conflicts such as the Israeli-Palestinian conflict have influenced US-Iran dynamics over time, and what role these conflicts play in shaping policy decisions.
  2. "Economic Diversification and Sanctions: Strategies for Isolating Iran's Economy" — Investigate Iran’s efforts to diversify its economic ties beyond oil and banking sectors, examining how such strategies have affected sanctions' effectiveness.
  3. "Mediation Efforts and their Impact on US-Iran Conflict Dynamics" — Evaluate the success of international mediation efforts in resolving the conflict between the US and Iran, including the JCPOA and more recent diplomatic initiatives under Biden’s administration.
  4. "Tackling Ideological Differences: Strategies for De-escalation and Durable Peace" — Propose specific strategies that could help mitigate ideological differences as a driving force behind the conflict, potentially leading to de-escalation and sustained peace.
