  • Iran's desire to develop its own nuclear capabilities as an insurance against potential threats.
  • The United States' goal of ensuring Iran does not obtain nuclear weapons.

Sanctions Regime: Sanctions on Iran are indeed multi-faceted and complex, targeting various sectors including banking, energy, oil exports, luxury goods imports, among others. These sanctions extend beyond just the nuclear issue:

  • Example: During the 2018 withdrawal from the JCPOA by the United States, there were additional economic sanctions reimposed on Iran.

Pivotal 2025-2026 Escalation: The 2025-2026 escalation is not a single event but rather a series of interconnected incidents that have led to ongoing tensions. Key factors contributing to escalations include:

  • Attacks by the Iranian Revolutionary Guards Corps (IRGC) against US military bases in Iraq.
  • Increased proxy conflicts involving groups such as Houthi rebels and ISIS militants.
